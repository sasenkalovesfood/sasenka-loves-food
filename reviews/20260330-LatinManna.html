I am a sucker for a good empanada. Always have been. And Latin Manna serves some of the best in Brisbane.

@latinmanna sits right on Mollison Street, directly across from West Village. Colourful front, busy road, plenty of foot traffic. And somehow people still walk straight past it. Do yourself a favour and don't.

I come here fairly regularly and I am always trying something new. This visit it was Manna's Paisa. One of Colombia's most iconic plates. Rice, beans, two eggs, patacón, arepa, sweet fried plantain, chorizo, pork belly, morcilla, beef mince, and avocado. All on one plate. I did not regret it.

Everything here is straightforward. You order on the app, you find your seat, and somehow you still get served with a smile. No theatre, no nonsense. Just good food and people who clearly care about what they're putting out.

That's Latin Manna.
